With preferred_departure='morning', no Malaga offer scores 100 on departure convenience — the dataset contains no morning Malaga services in the 12–14 July window. 
This is a property of the generated data, not the ranking: the generator assigned each company–destination pair 1–2 random departure windows, and none of the Malaga slots landed in the morning. 
In a live marketplace this would be a coverage gap; here it makes the departure criterion non-discriminating for this query, 
which the domination guard partly mitigates (departure weight is 15%, well below 60%).


The scoring model is parameterised, not fixed. The four weights are supplied at request time and validated by Weights.validate() before scoring. The formula Total = Σ wᵢ·sᵢ expands to whatever the traveller's weights define. 
The 40/25/15/20 split used in §5.4 is the specification's example configuration, used as a baseline; §8 demonstrates that changing the weights changes the winner, confirming the model reflects traveller priorities rather than an objective hierarchy.
